Salesforce KT – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

In short

Salesforce KT runs entirely inside your browser. It does not collect, store, sell or share your data with the developer or with any third party. There are no analytics, ads, tracking or telemetry.

About this extension

Salesforce KT is a Chrome extension that adds a developer and admin toolkit panel to Salesforce pages: record and field details, SOQL queries, REST API calls, debug logs, data import, user access checks and more. It works only on your own Salesforce org, using your own login and permissions.

Data the extension works with

When you use a tool, the extension loads records, fields, metadata, query results, debug logs and files you import from your Salesforce org, or sends the changes you request back to that same org. This data is processed in the page you are looking at. It is not copied to the developer or to any other place.

Files you choose to download (such as CSV, Excel, JSON, reports or backups) are saved by your browser to your own computer.

Your Salesforce session

To call the Salesforce API as you, the extension reads your org's existing session cookie at the moment of each request and sends it only to that same Salesforce org. The cookie is not stored, logged or shared. A request is never allowed to carry the session of one Salesforce org to a different host.

Information stored on your device

To remember your work, a few items are saved in your browser's local extension storage on your own computer, for example:

  • panel and button position and display options
  • org colour settings
  • SOQL query history and saved queries
  • API Explorer history, saved requests and variables
  • Apex Runner drafts and history
  • summary lists of debug logs

This information never leaves your device. Removing the extension deletes all of it.

API Explorer and external addresses

The only time a request goes to a site other than your Salesforce org is when you yourself type an https:// address in the API Explorer. That request goes only to the address you typed, with only the headers you entered, and without your Salesforce session.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect personal information, authentication information, website content, browsing history or user activity.
  • We do not sell or transfer user data to third parties.
  • We do not use or transfer user data for purposes unrelated to the extension's single purpose.
  • We do not use or transfer user data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.
  • We do not load or run remote code. All code is included in the extension package.

Permissions the extension asks for

  • activeTab and scripting: to show the panel on the Salesforce tab you open it from.
  • cookies and access to Salesforce domains (*.my.salesforce.com, *.lightning.force.com, *.my.salesforce-setup.com): to show the panel on Salesforce pages and to call your own org's API with your existing session.
  • storage: to save the local items listed above.
